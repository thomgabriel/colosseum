That cap is one for every caller together: one agent that makes 500 uses up the day for every other agent and person who would open a link, until the oldest is a day old (429). One nobody bought (no order or plan of a person names it) is deleted after 7 days, when the next is made, read by a partial index on `proposals(created_at) where from_link` (migration 0015).
