# Market Landscape
> Phase: discover | Brand: working-brand (unnamed) | Generated: 2026-10-01

Scope: goal-based and personalised yield; tokenized RWAs; stablecoin "earn" inside neobanks, fintechs and self-custody wallets; on-chain risk analytics for lending protocols and curators. Global and agentic framing, not Brazil-specific.

Verification key: **[V]** checked against a primary or near-primary source in this session. **[S]** secondary aggregator, treat as directional. **[U]** could not verify.

## 1. The four markets this brand sits across

### 1.1 Tokenized RWAs: big, concentrated, and only now being packaged
- Distributed RWA value (excluding stablecoins) was about **$38.8B on 3 Sept 2026** per RWA.xyz, as quoted by eco.com **[S]**. US Treasury products are still the largest share (around $16B in July 2026) **[S]**. Coverage describes growth as tripled but about 80% concentrated in one asset class (Treasuries) **[S]**.
- **Tokenized stocks went mainstream on Solana.** xStocks passed **$25B in total transaction volume**, including $3.5B on-chain, and reached 100 assets with a target of 500 by year-end. Kraken is finalising its acquisition of Backed, the issuer **[V, Kraken blog / The Block]**. Solana's tokenized-stock volume was $4.9B in H1 2026, up 6x on H2 2025 **[S]**. Solana carries more than 95% of tokenized-equity trading volume **[S]**.
- **Packaging is the 2026 move.** Ondo launched **Intelligent Portfolios** on 24 Sept 2026: single-token model portfolios based on BlackRock model strategies (BLKHIon, BLKDIGon, BLKGRWon), non-US only, with smart-contract rebalancing **[V, PR Newswire]**. Bitwise launched **Automated Token Portfolios (ATP)** in Aug 2026, with Glider rebalancing directly in each investor's wallet **[V, Glider blog]**.
- **What this means:** the market has gone from "here is a token" to "here is a basket". Every basket is still a **menu item**: a fixed model portfolio defined by the manager. Nobody is building the basket *backwards from one person's goal*. That gap is the brief's thesis, and the market has not closed it.

### 1.2 Stablecoin "earn" in neobanks, fintechs and wallets: commoditised and split by regulation
- The US **GENIUS Act** (July 2025) bars payment-stablecoin *issuers* from paying yield. Intermediaries still offer "rewards" through revenue-share, for example Coinbase USDC Rewards **[S, eco.com / stablecoininsider]**.
- That splits the market. US neobanks can't pay issuer yield, while **non-US neobanks advertise 5–10%** **[S]**. Juno pays about 5% on USDC **[S]**. The interface is always the same: **one "Earn X%" button**.
- Wealthfront's 2026 hero is literally **"Earn up to 4.45% APY / Better than a bank"** **[V, wealthfront.com]**. TradFi and crypto have converged on the same rate-led pitch.
- **What this means:** Rafael (Head of Product, secondary persona) is boxed in. Every competitor ships the identical button, and outside the US the rate is the only lever. A goal-based embed is a real product differentiator, *and it needs a brand that can recede inside his app*.

### 1.3 Goal-based and personalised investing: the expectation was set by robo-advisors
- Wealthfront's **Path** and Betterment's **goal forecaster** taught a generation to expect "am I on track?" with projections that update live **[V, Wealthfront support; Betterment goal-projection disclosure]**. Both still run *model portfolios behind a goal label*. The goal changes the glide path, not the instruments.
- **Nexa Finance** (Brazil, TradFi, B2B to advisors) is the cleanest articulation of the inverse. Its lines are *"The ideal product does not exist, yet. But you can build it."* and *"The market starts with the product. Nexa starts with the client."* Its product, "Nexa Genesis", is a real-time structuring engine **[V, nexafinance.com.br]**. It is positioned for bankers and wealth managers, not end users, and it is off-chain.
- **What this means:** "goal-based" is a known promise, so users expect it. "Made to measure for one goal, across on-chain RWAs, in your own wallet" is not occupied by anyone in this set.

### 1.4 On-chain risk analytics: the incumbents are moving away from neutral measurement
- **Chaos Labs exited Aave in April 2026.** It cited unprofitability, a methodology dispute over V4, and legal-liability concerns after a $27M oracle failure **[V, CoinDesk / The Block / Aave governance]**. Its 2026 homepage leads with *"Clarity Through Chaos"* and enterprise/"Sovereign AI" intelligence **[V, chaoslabs.xyz OG image]**.
- **Gauntlet pivoted to curation.** It has 30+ to 80+ curated vaults across Morpho, Kamino, Drift and Aera, and its hero is now *"Fuel your growth with onchain yield"*. It calls itself *"Crypto's premier yield curator"* **[V, gauntlet.xyz; S for vault counts]**.
- **What this means:** the two best-known risk brands now *sell yield* (Gauntlet) or *sell AI and oracles* (Chaos). That leaves a credibility gap for **independent, measured, reproducible liquidity data**: depth by notional × hour-of-week, which is what Priya (risk lead persona) asks for. The brand has to read as *neutral instrument, not vendor of yield*, on the risk surface.

## 2. The agentic shift (cross-cutting)

- **Robinhood opened to agents** in May 2026. Users can bring their own model (Claude, ChatGPT and others) into a sandboxed agent account. Cortex gives recommendations, and trades still need user confirmation **[V, Robinhood newsroom / TechCrunch / CNBC]**.
- On Solana, consumer "AI yield agents" are multiplying. **SAQTA** offers "deposit USDC, pick a strategy, walk away" and chases APY across Kamino, Marginfi and Drift **[S]**. **Yield AI** advertises about 4.88% APR stablecoin agents **[S]**. **Peaks** (Colosseum Frontier winner and accelerator) offers *self-managing portfolios based on a sector, personality, or worldview* **[V, Colosseum]**. **YieldCompass** (Frontier winner) is a search engine for *realized* APY with risk scoring **[V, Colosseum]**.
- The CFA Institute (2026) names the failure modes of "self-driving portfolios": cascade errors, false consensus, prompt drift and an **auditability gap**. It notes these systems *"deteriorate in high-volatility environments"* **[V, CFA Enterprising Investor]**.
- **What this means:** "agent" has become a commodity word. Most agent products sell **autonomy** ("walk away"). The brand's agent sells **accountability**. It is deterministic, its constraints can be edited, every action is logged with an explorer link, and it re-trues the structure rather than chasing APY. That contrast is the most defensible agentic story available, and it fits the joiner metaphor exactly.

## 3. Trajectory (next 6–12 months, opinionated)

1. **Model portfolios on-chain will multiply** (Ondo, Bitwise/Glider, Coinbase Equities). Packaging stops being a differentiator and *fit* becomes one.
2. **Tokenized equities and commodities will be used as lending collateral.** Liquidation-depth risk (the "3am Sunday" problem) becomes a governance topic. Data that can't be backfilled (collected since Sept 2026) gains value every week.
3. **Regulators and compliance teams will ask agents to explain themselves.** EU AI Act explainability, and Robinhood's confirmation gate as the consumer norm. Provenance on every number becomes a *table-stakes trust feature*, not a nerd detail.
4. **"Earn" stays a rate war** outside the US. Partners need a reason to embed something that isn't a bigger number.

## 4. Expectation shifts by persona

| Persona | Old expectation | 2026 expectation | Brand implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mariana 1a** (crypto-native) | Highest APY, I'll manage it | Self-custody + proof: sources, timestamps, explorer links. Distrusts leaderboards after the 2–5.6x overstatement era | Provenance must be *visible design*, not a tooltip. No leaderboards, no "up to X%" |
| **Mariana 1b** (life-goal saver) | A savings rate | "Am I on track?" in plain words (Wealthfront Path set the bar), plus "what if" up front | On-track language first, number second. Calm, no gamification |
| **Rafael** (neobank PM) | A yield API | A *differentiated* earn feature that compliance can defend and that won't embarrass the app | White-label that recedes. Docs as a brand surface. Mocks visibly labelled |
| **Priya** (risk lead) | Vendor risk reports | Reproducible, versioned, sample-counted measurements from someone not selling yield | Dashboard language of a measuring instrument: hairlines, units, n=, version stamps |

## 5. Implications for the brand (decisions, not options)

- **Position against the menu, not against competitors.** Ondo, Glider, Bitwise, Wealthfront and Betterment all sell *fixed portfolios with nicer packaging*. The brand's enemy is the menu itself, which is already the brief's POV, and the market confirms it.
- **Own "fit + visible joints" as a pair.** Nexa owns "build the ideal product" (TradFi, B2B), and nobody on-chain does. Nobody in the set treats *provenance as visual identity*.
- **Make the agent a craftsman, never a pilot.** Glider glides, Peaks climbs and SAQTA "walks away". Motion and aspiration metaphors are the category default. A joiner who measures, cuts and re-trues is the open lane.
- **The risk layer must look like it measures, not like it sells.** That is the opening Gauntlet and Chaos Labs left.
