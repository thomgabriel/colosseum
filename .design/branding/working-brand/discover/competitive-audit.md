# Competitive Audit
> Phase: discover | Brand: working-brand (unnamed) | Generated: 2026-10-01

Eight brands from the brief. Visual evidence comes from each brand's live homepage, official brand kit and Open Graph card, fetched 2026-10-01. Palettes were sampled from those assets, not guessed. **[V]** verified, **[S]** secondary, **[U]** unverified.

---

## 1. Brand-by-brand

### Glider (glider.fi): non-custodial automated portfolios, B2C + wallet API
- **Positioning:** *"Portfolio meets automation."* The meta description is *"Invest in US stocks, gold and crypto with automated portfolios that rebalance on your schedule. Start with $1 and keep assets in your own account."* It is the infrastructure behind Bitwise ATPs ("Direct-to-Wallet Strategies") **[V]**.
- **Naming:** a motion metaphor (glider = effortless flight). Features are named in category jargon: "strategies", "ATP", "Direct-to-Wallet".
- **Visual:** mint-green ground (#BBF2D6 sampled from OG card) with black text, a geometric sans wordmark in caps plus a small bird/wing mark. The hero image is a **3D glass toggle switch with crypto coin icons inside**, which is exactly the "3D coins / glassy" fintech trope **[V, OG image]**.
- **Voice:** short, product-led, confident. The blog is measured and candid about Reg S limits **[V]**.
- **Strength / weakness:** the closest product analogue (non-custodial, policy rebalancing, B2B2C). But the brand is generic "friendly crypto app". The *strategy is the manager's*, not the user's.

### Ondo Finance / Ondo Intelligent Portfolios: tokenized RWAs and now model-portfolio tokens
- **Positioning:** *"Welcome to the Open Economy."* *"Institutional-grade platforms, assets, and infrastructure to bring financial markets onchain."* Intelligent Portfolios are tokens built on "BlackRock model strategies" **[V]**.
- **Naming:** a coined two-syllable vowel-ending word (Ondo). Products use tickers with an "-on" suffix (USDY, OUSG, BLKHIon), plus "Nexus" for infrastructure and "Intelligent" as the AI-adjacent adjective **[V]**.
- **Visual:** the 2025 identity uses a custom **Ondo Sans** (based on Gelix, open apertures), black/white plus "warm, classic tones with bold colour pops", and **cinematic documentary street photography** (Manhattan Bridge archway, people walking). Motion themes are "opening aperture" and "unmasking" **[V, Ondo blog Feb 2025; OG image]**.
- **Voice:** institutional, mission-led ("tool to help improve things in the real world for real people").
- **Audit note:** the brief's anti-pattern says "Ondo-style blue gradients". **Ondo's current identity is not blue gradients**; it is warm documentary photography on black. The blue/indigo-gradient look belongs to Betterment, Gauntlet and Chaos Labs (below). Strategy should restate the anti-pattern as **"category blue/indigo gradients"** so it targets the right thing. Ondo's *real* overlap risk with this brand is **warm tones on black plus "openness" as a transparency metaphor**. We differentiate by showing *material and mechanism* (cut wood, joints) where Ondo shows *cities and people*.

### Peaks (peaks.trade): Solana, agentic thematic portfolios
- **Positioning:** *"The trading app for ideas, trends and tokens."* On Colosseum: *"investing in any idea… self-managing portfolios based on a sector, personality, or worldview and then share them with the world"*. Frontier hackathon consumer winner, Colosseum accelerator **[V]**.
- **Naming:** an aspiration/summit metaphor. Also note **name collision**: Peaks.com is a Dutch ETF app.
- **Visual:** near-black UI (#0A0B0D theme colour), a white **mountain-range icon**, P&L green #16C568 and red #B54444, set in Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter and Geist Mono **[V, page source / OG card]**. It reads as a standard dark trading app.
- **Voice:** trader-energy, social ("share them with the world").
- **Strength / weakness:** strong distribution story (Colosseum). It is *idea-driven speculation*, the opposite of goal-driven fit. It is the most likely judge comparison at Colosseum, so we must look nothing like a trading app.

### Gauntlet (gauntlet.xyz): risk manager turned yield curator
- **Positioning:** *"Fuel your growth with onchain yield."* *"Crypto's premier yield curator."* Risk framed as "quantitative, model-driven frameworks with human oversight" **[V]**.
- **Naming:** a combat/challenge metaphor. Products: Gauntlet Vaults, USD Alpha, Prime, Frontier, Aera, VaultBook.
- **Visual (official brand kit):** Gauntlet Purple #7180F5, Off Black #1E1C2D, Off White #FBFAFF, G Pink #FF8099. The gradient runs #1E1C2D → #6C77F2 → #D1CBFF. Type is **PP Telegraph** (headers) + **Söhne** + **Söhne Mono**. A pixel-stepped "G" mark **[V, brand kit]**.
- **Voice:** institutional authority ("8+ years managing risk for the largest DeFi protocols").
- **Weakness for Priya:** now a *seller of yield*, so its risk data is no longer neutral.

### Chaos Labs (chaoslabs.xyz): risk, oracles (Edge), now "Sovereign AI"
- **Positioning:** *"Clarity Through Chaos."* *"Turns complex, fragmented data into decision-ready, institutional-grade intelligence."* The 2026 OG card reads "Building the Future of Sovereign AI". It exited Aave in Apr 2026 **[V]**.
- **Naming:** an oxymoron/brand-paradox name with a "Labs" suffix.
- **Visual (official brand kit):** **Chaos Blue #2D6AE7**, dark gradient #25354B → #0B101C, grey ramp #F2F4F7 to #0C111D. Type is **At Aero** (headlines), **Aeonik Fono** (subheads), **Inter** (body). The 2025 rebrand is by Wednesday Studio, built on geometric line systems **[V, brand kit; rebrand.gallery]**.
- **Voice:** enterprise, controlled, intelligence-led.
- **Note:** their geometric "fan of lines" is the closest the category gets to diagram language. It is abstract vector, not material.

### Wealthfront: robo-advisor, the expectation-setter for "goals" (Path)
- **Positioning:** *"Earn up to 4.45% APY. Better than a bank."* OG card: *"Money works better here."* **[V]**.
- **Naming:** a compound coined name (wealth + front). Plain-descriptive product names (Cash Account, Automated Investing Account, Path).
- **Visual:** **indigo/violet** (#4A46BD, #352770 sampled), a heavy grotesk paired with a **serif italic** ("better here."), and phone mockups with balances **[V, OG image]**.
- **Voice:** confident, rate-led, simple.

### Betterment: robo-advisor, goal visualisation
- **Positioning:** *"Build wealth with confidence and ease."* *"Betterment automates the work for you."* Goals framed as life milestones ("next year's vacation", "down payment") **[V]**.
- **Naming:** an abstract improvement noun.
- **Visual:** **royal-to-navy blue gradient** (#195DD0 → #041762), a **yellow rising-sun mark**, and a rounded geometric sans. Aspirational lifestyle photography **[V, OG image]**.
- **Voice:** friendly reassurance, "you're in control".

### Nexa Finance: TradFi hyper-personalisation reference (Brazil, B2B to advisors)
- **Positioning:** *"Hyperpersonalized investments in real time."* *"The ideal product does not exist, yet. But you can build it."* *"The market starts with the product. Nexa starts with the client."* Its engine is Nexa Genesis **[V]**.
- **Naming:** a coined Latin-ish "nex-" (nexus/next) with an "-a" ending. The product name is mythic-generic (Genesis).
- **Visual:** **deep green #002F28**, sage #80B7A4, **lime #C4F29B**, mist #E4EEE9, teal #006D5A, set in **Satoshi**. Advisor/client headshots plus portfolio visualisations **[V, page source]**.
- **Voice:** consultative, clear, "we take on the complexity".
- **Takeaway:** Nexa owns the *story* we admire, but in TradFi, for advisors, with a conventional fintech look. We can own the same inversion **on-chain, for the end user, with the joints visible**.

## 2. Positioning map

Axes per methodology. **Conservative ↔ Progressive** = offer and risk posture. **Traditional ↔ Modern** = visual language.

```
                              MODERN (digital-abstract)
                                       |
              Wealthfront ●            |            ● Glider (mint, 3D glass)
                                       |      ● Peaks (dark trading app)
          Betterment ●                 |   ● Chaos Labs (blue lines)
                         Gauntlet ●    |
CONSERVATIVE ──────────────────────────┼────────────────────────── PROGRESSIVE
                                       |  ● Ondo (doc. photography)
                    Nexa ● (green, headshots)
                                       |
                                       |              ◎ WHITE SPACE
                                       |     progressive offer + material,
                                       |     crafted, mechanism-visible look
                              TRADITIONAL (material / crafted)
```

A third axis explains the gap better than the two required ones. **Every competitor sits on the "digital-abstract" side** (gradients, renders, vector lines, UI mockups). **None is "material-mechanical"**: real objects, real joints, the mechanism photographed. The lower-right quadrant is empty, and that is where the brand should go: a progressive offer in a crafted, material visual language. It must stay *modern in execution* (hairlines, grid, data) so it doesn't slide into "traditional = old".

## 3. Category visual patterns (what to avoid by default)

| Dimension | Category default (observed) | Count | Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Colour** | Blue/indigo/violet as primary | 4 of 8 (Betterment, Wealthfront, Gauntlet, Chaos) | Avoid any blue-violet primary |
| | Green/mint/lime | 2 of 8 (Glider, Nexa), plus P&L green (Peaks) | Avoid mint and lime. Green only as a semantic "on track" |
| | Warm material tones | 0 of 8 (Ondo has warm *photography*, not a warm palette) | **Open** |
| **Type** | Geometric/neo-grotesk sans (Söhne, PP Telegraph, At Aero, Inter, Satoshi, Plus Jakarta, Gelix) | 8 of 8 | A sans is unavoidable for UI. Pick one *not* on this list, and add a non-sans voice for warmth |
| | Serif accent | 1 (Wealthfront italic) | A serif is available, but not italic-flourish |
| **Imagery** | Phone/UI mockups, 3D renders, abstract geometry, headshots, cityscapes | all | **Photographed physical mechanism (wood, joints) is unoccupied** |
| **Marks** | Letter monograms (G, stylised O), nature icons (sun, bird, mountain) | 6 of 8 | Avoid nature-icon marks (sun/peak/wing) and letter-in-shape monograms |
| **Data display** | Big APY numbers, balance hero | Wealthfront, Glider, Peaks | Never lead with a rate. Lead with *the goal and whether it holds* |
| **Provenance** | Absent from brand language. At best "transparency into constituents" (Ondo) | 0 treat it visually | **Open: make provenance a brand element** |

## 4. Naming patterns in the category (for strategy to avoid)

| Pattern | Examples | Why to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Motion / ascent / aspiration metaphors** | Glider, Peaks, Wealth*front*, Better*ment*, Drift, Jupiter, Hive | Overcrowded. Sells autonomy and "up". Our story is fit and holding, not flight |
| **Coined vowel-ending 2-syllable words** | Ondo, Nexa, Aave, Morpho, Juno, Kamino, Euler | Interchangeable, forgettable, hard to own in search |
| **Combat / adversity / paradox** | Gauntlet, Chaos Labs (also Sentinel/Guardian-type names common in risk) | Cold and defensive, the opposite of "warm, capable" |
| **Suffix crutches** | -Labs, -Finance, -Capital, -fi, .xyz, -AI | Signals crypto-native template naming. Rafael would hesitate to embed it |
| **"Intelligent / AI / Agent / Yield" descriptors** | Ondo *Intelligent* Portfolios, *Yield* AI, *Yield*Compass, SAQTA | Commodity words in 2026. Ages instantly |
| **Mythic-generic** | Genesis (Nexa), Nexus (Ondo), Prime/Frontier/Alpha (Gauntlet vaults) | Zero distinctiveness |
| **Japanese craft words already in use** | **Kigumi** is taken by a crypto-fiat services company (kigumi.xyz) **[V]**. **Tsugite** is a well-known U-Tokyo joinery-design software (UIST '20) **[V]** | Direct collision, and an exoticism risk |
| **Joinery English words already in use** | **Dovetail** (Australian research SaaS, plus at least two London fintechs) **[V]**. **Truss** (construction fintech) **[V]**. **Lattice** (HR software, semiconductor) **[U, common knowledge]** | Crowded. Check every candidate |

**Rule for strategy:** prefer a **short, verb-able or tool-like English word, or an invented word built from craft/measure roots**, that is pronounceable in EN/PT/ES, isn't a motion metaphor, doesn't end in "-a/-o" by default, and needs no suffix.

## 5. White space (the decision)

1. **Positioning white space: "made to measure, with the joints showing."** Competitors split into *menus with nice packaging* (Ondo, Glider/Bitwise, Wealthfront, Betterment), *speculation* (Peaks), and *risk vendors that now sell yield or AI* (Gauntlet, Chaos). Nobody on-chain claims **fit to one person's goal** together with **every assumption visible**. Nexa claims fit, but off-chain and for advisors.
2. **Visual white space: material mechanism.** The category is 100% digital-abstract. Photographed timber joinery, exploded-view diagrams and hairline assembly drawings on warm black or daylight paper are unclaimed. Warm wood tones are unclaimed as a *palette* (Ondo only uses them in photographs).
3. **Trust white space: provenance as identity.** No competitor treats source / fetched_at / method as a visual signature. Turning "the pin" into the provenance glyph on every number makes the brief's non-negotiable the most recognisable brand element.
4. **Agent white space: accountable craftsman, not autopilot.** Category agents "walk away", "glide" or "self-manage". Ours *measures, proposes, cuts, logs and re-trues*. Nobody in the set uses this framing.
5. **Risk-layer white space: neutral instrument.** With Gauntlet curating and Chaos pivoting, a risk product that *looks like a measuring instrument* (versioned, n=, method in the open) and visibly sells no yield has room. **Caution:** "instrument" is Teiten's territory visually (chassis, nameplates). Express it through *drawing conventions* (dimension lines, section hatch, tolerances), never through hardware skeuomorphism.
