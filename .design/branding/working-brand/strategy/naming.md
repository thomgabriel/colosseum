# Naming
> Phase: strategy | Brand: working-brand (unnamed) | Generated: 2026-10-01

**Status: recommendation only. The founder decides. No name is adopted in any other strategy file, which all keep `[Name]`.**

---

## 1. Brief and criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Length | Short. Ideally 1–2 syllables, about 8 letters max |
| Languages | Easy to say and spell in **EN, PT and ES**. **No bad meaning** in any of them |
| Archetype | Sage × Caregiver: warm and trustworthy. Not techy-cold, not cute |
| Meaning | Evokes fit, measure, truth or joinery. A Japanese word is allowed only if easy and not a cliché. No kanji in the logotype |
| Category hygiene | Not generic fintech: no "-ly", "-fi", "yield", "vault", "earn", "-Labs", "AI". No motion or ascent metaphors (Glider, Peaks). No combat names (Gauntlet) |
| Embed | Works as **"powered by [Name]"** next to a partner's brand |
| Known collisions | Kigumi (kigumi.xyz, crypto-fiat), Tsugite (U-Tokyo joinery software), Dovetail (research SaaS + fintechs), Truss (construction fintech) |

**Territories (from discover):** 1 joint and pin · 2 true / plumb / square · 3 fit and measure · 4 structure and load path · 5 grain and species. The lead was 1 × 2.

## 5. Recommended top 3

### 1. Tenon (recommended)
*"The piece cut to fit one place, with its end and its pin left showing."* It is the only name that says **made to measure** and **every joint shown** at once. It's English, so craft comes without exoticism. Easy in all three languages, no meaning to trip over in PT or ES, no finance namesake found.
**Evidence:** no finance or crypto "Tenon" found **[V, search]**. tenon.fi unregistered **[V, whois]**. tenon.finance and tenon.money no registration found **[S]**. tenon.xyz for sale **[V]**. @tenon dormant-taken **[V]**. @tenonfinance and @tenon_xyz no active account **[S]**. Trademarks unchecked **[U]**.
**Suggested identity kit:** tenon.finance (primary, if standard price) plus tenon.fi (redirect), with a price check on tenon.xyz. X handle @tenonfinance or @tenon_xyz.

### 2. Masu
*"A wooden measure for value that also bears the load."* The deepest story (rice-as-money measure, hinoki, bracket-set bearing block), soft and easy everywhere. It costs a Japanese word and a "standard measure" counter-reading.
**Evidence:** no fintech "Masu" found **[V, search]**. MASU fashion brand **[V]**. masu.com for sale **[V]**. masu.finance, masu.money and getmasu.com no registration found **[S]**. @masu no active account found **[S]**. Trademarks unchecked **[U]**.

### 3. Cerne
*"Heartwood: the strongest part of the wood, the core of the matter."* The warmest, most Caregiver name, and a real word in PT and ES. It costs a split pronunciation in EN and doesn't say "fit".
**Evidence:** no finance "Cerne" found **[V, search]**. Brazilian IT SME namesakes **[V]**. cerne.fi unregistered **[V, whois]**. cerne.finance no registration found **[S]**. @cerne taken **[V]**. @cerne_xyz and @cernefinance no active account **[S]**. ES meaning **[U]**. Trademarks unchecked **[U]**.

**Strategist's call:** **Tenon.** The brand is "made to measure, every joint shown", and Tenon is that sentence as an object. Masu is the better story but a weaker fit to the positioning. Cerne is the warmer word but a weaker global name.

## 6. Risk-product noun (branded house: [Name] Bearing)

| Candidate | Logic | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Bearing** | *Bearing capacity* is the load a structure or ground can carry before it fails. That is the risk lead's question about collateral: how much can be sold before price gives way. In joinery, the bearing surface is where load passes through a joint | **Recommended.** "Tenon Bearing", "Masu Bearing" and "Cerne Bearing" all read well. Masu *is* a bearing block, so that pairing is the most coherent |
| Depth | The market's own word for liquidity | **Fallback.** Clear to Priya but generic; it doesn't build brand |
| Load Path | Research's structural term | Two words, engineering-heavy |
| Loadline / Plimsoll | The ship's safe-load mark | Strong metaphor, but maritime. It mixes the material language |
| Span, Corbel, Lintel | Structural members | Cold, or taken (Span.io, Corbel font **[U]**) |

**Risks for Bearing:** the crypto ear may hear "bear" or "bearish". Mitigate by always using the full product name, never shortening to "Bear-", and using "bearing capacity" in the descriptor line. @bearing on X is taken (80 followers) **[V]**. That's irrelevant for a product noun under the masterbrand's handle.

**Descriptor line:** *"[Name] Bearing: measured exit capacity for on-chain collateral, by size, path and hour of week."*

## Decision (2026-10-01): provisional name **Tenonfi**
The founder chose **Tenonfi** as the **provisional** name. It's Tenon plus "-fi", and it isn't final yet.
- **Why it works:** it keeps the Tenon meaning (cut to fit one mortise, with the joint visible) and the "-fi" ending helps handle and domain availability (e.g. tenonfi.com, @tenonfi).
- **Trade-off:** "-fi" was on this file's list of generic fintech suffixes. If it's dropped later, the name falls back to plain **Tenon**, which keeps the same identity.
- **Checks still required before it's final:** native-speaker checks in PT and ES; trademark searches in USPTO, INPI and EUIPO (classes 36/42); domain and X handle secured. **None of these has been done for "Tenonfi".**
- **For downstream phases:** identity uses "Tenonfi" for the wordmark exploration but must keep the mark usable as plain "Tenon". The folder slug stays `working-brand` until the name is final.
