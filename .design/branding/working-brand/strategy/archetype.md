# Archetype
> Phase: strategy | Brand: working-brand ([Name] TBD, see naming.md) | Generated: 2026-10-01

## Decision (founder-confirmed)

**Primary: The Sage (about 60%). Secondary: The Caregiver (about 40%).**

- **The Sage leads** as the trusted adviser who explains *why the plan holds*. Its core desire is understanding, and its gift to the user is "you can see how it works".
- **The Caregiver qualifies everything the Sage says.** The Sage's knowledge is always pointed at *one person's goal*, delivered warmly, and on the lookout for them ("we'd tell you in March, not in August").
- Mark & Pearson list **Caregiver + Sage** as a classic pairing (Mayo Clinic: expert care). We use it on purpose. The proposition is expert care for someone's money and plans, with the workings shown.

> **In one line:** a master joiner who works for you. They measure you first, cut the pieces for your goal alone, show you every joint, and come back to re-true it when the wood moves.

---

## Where the rest of the brand idea lives

The founder chose Sage × Caregiver over Creator × Caregiver. That has two consequences that need to be written down so they aren't lost:

1. **"Made to measure" is carried by positioning and messaging, not by the archetype.** The Creator would have carried "made to measure" for free. The Sage doesn't. So every surface states the fit explicitly: *the goal comes first, and the portfolio is built from it.* The tagline, the headline and the plan-view hero must all name the fit. See messaging.md, where "made to measure" is the first supporting message and is never optional.
2. **Joinery means understanding first, and craft is how that understanding is expressed.** In a Creator brand, joinery would mean "look how beautifully we make things". In this Sage brand it means **"you can see how it works"**: the exploded view, the visible pin, the through-tenon whose end shows on the outside face. The craft is real, but it serves the explanation. A visual that is beautiful but explains nothing fails the brand.

## The tension: Sage is crowded, so here's how we stand apart

**Naming it plainly:** four of the eight benchmarked brands already sound like explainers. Betterment explains your forecast, Wealthfront explains your Path, Gauntlet explains its models, and Chaos Labs explains its intelligence. A generic Sage brand would be one more voice in that room. A Sage that sounds like a quant desk or a forecasting chart is invisible here.

**How we resolve it, in three moves. All three are required:**

| # | Move | Category Sage does | Our Sage does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Caregiver warmth: talk about the person, not the market** | "Markets moved 2% this week." "Our models indicate…" | "Your apartment fund is on track." Knowledge is always addressed to one goal by name. The market is only mentioned when it touches *your* plan |
| 2 | **Candour about the downside, stated first** | Explains why the product is good. Downside lives in disclosures | Explains **when the plan would fail, and what we'd do**. Stress cases sit beside the base case, not in a footnote. "If rates fall by a third, you'd land in September instead, and we'd tell you in March." The Sage's truth includes the bad news |
| 3 | **Material, not abstract** | Knowledge shown as gradients, charts and dashboards (blue/indigo, 4 of 8) | Knowledge shown as **a real joint you can open**. The exploded view, the pin, the hatch. Understanding you can almost touch |

**The test:** if a sentence could appear unchanged on Betterment's or Gauntlet's site, it is the crowded Sage. Rewrite it until it names a person's goal, a downside, or a visible joint.

## Shadow traits to avoid

### Sage shadows
| Shadow | How it would show up here | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| **Ivory tower** | Lecturing about "credit-aware haircuts" before saying whether her goal lands | Answer first ("On track · June 2028"), workings one tap away |
| **Analysis paralysis / provenance clutter** | Every number buried under source chips, the screen unreadable | The **pin** is small and quiet. Details open on demand. Provenance is always available but never shouted |
| **Cold superiority** | "As any structurer knows…" | Expert, never elitist. We explain without implying the user should already know |
| **False omniscience** | Presenting projections as knowledge | Projections are labelled as projections. Measured, estimated and MOCK are visually distinct. "I don't know yet" is an allowed sentence |

### Caregiver shadows
| Shadow | How it would show up here | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| **Paternalism** | "Don't worry, we'll handle everything" | The user owns the decision. The agent **proposes and explains**. The constraint sheet is editable. The wallet is theirs |
| **False reassurance** | "You're all set!" when the plan is fragile | Warmth never overrides candour. Reassure with facts ("you have a week's access to cash any time"), never with tone alone |
| **Smothering / nagging** | Constant nudges, streaks, "check in today!" | Speak when something changes the goal. Silence is a feature |
| **Guilt-tripping** | "You're falling behind on your dream" | Off-track states are stated neutrally, with the options |

### Agent-specific shadows (from the category)
- **Autopilot.** "Set and forget", "walk away" and "self-driving" are banned. The agent is accountable: it measures, proposes, explains, logs and re-trues.
- **Mascot.** No face, no avatar, no name-with-personality, no cute character. The agent speaks in first person about what it did. It is a role, not a character.
- **Magician creep.** No "AI magic", "intelligent", "smart" or "unlock". The engine is deterministic, and we say so.

## Communication style

**How this Sage × Caregiver speaks:**
- **Answer, then reason, then risk.** "You're on track for June 2028 (answer). Most of it sits in short-dated treasuries, which is why it holds when rates move (reason). If rates fall by a third, you'd land in September instead (risk)."
- **To one person, about one goal.** Second person, the goal by its name, the date and the amount.
- **Numbers carry their origin.** "4.6% after haircut, from [source], as of 14:02 UTC" (illustrative). The pin does this visually, and the sentence does it when spoken.
- **The downside is said out loud and early**, with what we'll do about it.
- **First person for actions, plainly.** "I moved a little into treasuries." Accountability, not personality.
- **Short sentences, no jargon on the first layer.** Technical terms such as haircut, depth and slippage are welcome one layer down, and defined once.
- **Calm.** No urgency, no exclamation marks, no hype. The Sage's confidence comes from showing the work, and the Caregiver's warmth comes from attention, not adjectives.

See voice-and-tone.md for the operational rules.

## Visual tendencies

Archetype defaults: Sage = clean typography, structured layouts, data visualisation, muted palettes. Caregiver = soft colours, calming palettes, nurturing imagery, rounded shapes. **We take the Sage's structure and the Caregiver's warmth, and we decline the Caregiver's rounded softness.** Joinery is square.

| Archetype trait | How it lands in the "Open Joint" direction (discover/mood-board-direction.md) |
|---|---|
| Sage: structure, clarity | Lattice grid, hairline rules, exploded-view and dimension-line drawings, tabular figures, the pin as provenance glyph |
| Sage: truth made visible | Through-joints and the visible pin. MOCK shown as section hatch plus the word MOCK, so a mock never looks like finished timber |
| Caregiver: warmth | Two wood species (hinoki pale, hardwood dark) on warm black or daylight paper. Never cold navy, never pure white |
| Caregiver: calm | Generous negative space on consumer surfaces. One goal and one sentence above the fold. Slide-and-lock motion with no bounce, no confetti |
| Caregiver: attention to the person | The serif display voice used only for the personal line ("Your apartment fund is on track"). Photography shows hands fitting pieces, never faces staring at phones |
| Not Caregiver-rounded | Radius 0–2px on brand surfaces. Softness comes from colour temperature and light, not from blobs or pills |

### Style base connection (`humanist-literary`, `minimal-dark`, `swiss-minimalist`)
Custom tokens borrow structure only, and every preset's accent colour is dropped.
- **`humanist-literary` ↔ Caregiver.** Its warm paper ground and serif/sans split give the personal register ("a letter about your goal"). Its terracotta accent is dropped because it sits in cinnabar/Teiten territory.
- **`minimal-dark` ↔ Sage at depth.** Layered warm darkness for the risk dashboard and dark mode, matching the quiet authority of a measuring instrument. Its glass cards, cool black and amber accent are dropped. Hinoki and hardwood replace the accent.
- **`swiss-minimalist` ↔ Sage's structure.** Grid discipline and typographic hierarchy for API docs and the embed, where Rafael judges quality by docs. Its red and blue accents are dropped.

The blend works because each preset maps to one register of the archetype: Caregiver warmth (literary), Sage depth (dark), and Sage structure (Swiss). The joinery language (pin, tenon, hatch, exploded view) is what makes them one brand.
