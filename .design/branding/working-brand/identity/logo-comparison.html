Provenance glyph (shared by all directions)

The pin seen through the tenon end, in line mode. Live = solid pin. Stale = hollow pin + the word "stale". MOCK = section hatch, no pin + the word MOCK.

A · Construction

Master on a 32-unit grid. H = post height = 28u. Post width 10u = 0.36H is the base measure: clear space and lockup gap. Heights post : rail : tenon = 7 : 3 : 2. Pin d = tenon height ÷ 2, offset 7u / 5u toward the end grain.

Cuts, lockups, minimums

Three masters, never auto-scaled across a breakpoint: master ≥ 32 px, 24 hint 22–31 px, small cut 12–21 px.
