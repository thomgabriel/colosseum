# Brand Applications
> Phase: identity | Brand: Tenonfi (provisional; fallback "Tenon") | Generated: 2026-10-01 | Rewritten 2026-10-08 on `IDENTITY-2` (docs/GATES.md)

All figures below are **illustrative**. In the product, every yield, price and FX figure carries the pin (source · fetched_at · method), and sample figures carry the hatch with the card's quiet line "Sample figures" (gate `MOCK-QUIET`: never a boxed MOCK word). The pin is shown here as ⊡.

**One test for every surface:** can you see what it's for (the goal), why it holds (the joints), and where each number came from (the pin)?

---

## 1. App goal card (consumer, both modes)

- **Ground** `night` or `paper`. The card is `night-2` / white with a 1 px `line` / `line-l` border and **10 px radius**. No shadow.
- **Eyebrow (caption):** "Apartment fund · Jun 2028" in Inter 500, uppercase, muted.
- **Amount:** "$12,480 ⊡" in Inter Tight 600, 28 px, tabular. The pin in honey / honey-l.
- **One line, Inter 400, muted:** "6.4% net ⊡ · cash in 7 days".
- **Status pill:** square glyph + "On track" on a 14% leaf tint. Watch = half square on clay; off track = notched outline on madder. Always the word.
- **Progress bar:** honey on `line`, pill ends.
- **One action:** "See your plan" as a honey / honey-l underlined link, or the one primary button of the view (honey, ink text).
- **Not here:** photography, patterns, leaderboards, a big APY as the headline, progress rings.

## 2. Plan view (consumer, both modes)

- **Hero:** the one sentence to the person in Inter Tight 600 ("Your apartment fund is on track."), then answer → reason → risk (voice-and-tone.md) in three short Inter lines. A faint honey glow behind it.

## 4. API docs (`/docs`, day default, night available)

- **Layout:** two columns. Prose in Inter on the left; request and response examples in Plex Mono on the right on `paper-3` / `night-3` with 10 px radius.
- **Provenance in the schema:** every numeric example shows its `source`, `fetched_at` and `method` fields, and the pin annotates them in rendered examples.
- **Mock examples:** hatched code-block gutter + `"provenance": "mock"` highlighted + the line "Sample figures" under the block. Sandbox examples add "· test network".
- **Disclaimer:** the same constant, in a hairline box at the top of the reference section.
- **Header:** mark + `tenonfi` + "API" in Inter 500. No photography.

## 5. Tenonfi Analytics dashboard (night default)

- **Register:** Meteora's density with our joints. `night` ground, `night-2` tiles with 1 px `line` and 10 px radius, 36 px rows, 13 px type. No bevels, no nameplates, no LEDs (anti-Teiten).
- **Type:** Inter for cells and heads, Inter Tight 600 for KPIs, Plex Mono for meta lines.
- **Every metric:** value ⊡ with a signed delta beneath (leaf up, madder down) · unit · n= · method version · as-of (UTC). Example: "TSLAx · $412K sellable at ≤ 1% cost ⊡ +18% · Sun 03:00 UTC · n=412 · method v1.3".
- **Rows:** asset avatar + name (600) + type chip (pill) · sparkline coloured by direction · tabular values · status cell (pin, stale, MOCK). Selected row on `honey-tint`. Filter chips are pills; the selected chip is honey text on honey tint.
- **Hero visual:** the hour-of-week depth **heatmap** (24 × 7) on the night → honey → pale ramp; no-sample cells empty with an en dash.
- **Lockup:** mark + `tenonfi Analytics`, top left, at a modest size.
