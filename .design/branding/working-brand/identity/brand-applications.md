# Brand Applications
> Phase: identity | Brand: Tenonfi (provisional; fallback "Tenon") | Generated: 2026-10-01

All figures below are **illustrative**. In the product, every yield, price and FX figure carries the pin glyph (source · fetched_at · method), and mocks carry hatch + MOCK. The pin is shown here as ⊡.

**One test for every surface:** can you see what it's for (the goal), why it holds (the joints), and where each number came from (the pin)?

---

## 1. App goal card (consumer, light default)

- **Ground** `paper`. The card is `paper-raised` with a 1 px `hair` border and **0–2 px radius**. No shadow.
- **First line, serif (Newsreader 400):** "Your apartment fund is on track." This is the only serif on the screen.
- **Status:** a solid `forest` square + "On track · June 2028" in Plex Sans 500. Watch = half-filled `ochre` square; off track = notched `madder` outline. Always the word.
- **Below, in Plex Sans:** "$12,480 of $40,000 ⊡ · access to cash within 7 days". Tabular figures.
- **One action:** "See your plan" in `hardwood` text with an underline (a link, not a pill).
- **Not here:** photography, patterns, leaderboards, a big APY number, progress-ring gamification.

## 2. Plan view (consumer, light and dark)

- **Hero:** the goal sentence in serif, then answer → reason → risk (voice-and-tone.md) in three short sans lines.
- **The plan drawing (H3, orthographic):** post with mortise (goal dimensions), tenons as legs with square callouts. Each callout reads: leg · weight · after-haircut yield ⊡ · "quoted 9.1%" in `stone`.
- **Stress cases** sit next to the base case as dashed lines, never in a tab or footnote.
- **Exit plan panel (J3):** "Access to cash" with the dashed lift-out dimension: "up to $4,000 within a day · the rest within 7 days · cost ≤ 0.5% ⊡".
- **"Why this plan?"** opens the exploded view (pieces part on their axes, callouts fade in).
- **Disclaimer:** rendered from the `DISCLAIMER` constant in a hairline-boxed block directly under the plan, in Plex Sans 400 at body size. Not a footer, not grey micro-text.
- **Activity:** each line reads "I moved $1,200 from USDC to USDY because… ⊡ · Tx ↗" with the explorer link in Plex Mono.

## 3. Partner embed (white-label)

- **Recedes completely:** the partner's colours, font, radius and button style.
- **Survives (non-negotiable):** the pin glyph and its provenance popover (Plex Mono, or the partner's mono), the section hatch + **MOCK**, hairline structure in the plan drawing, the disclaimer block, explorer links.
- **Credit:** at the foot of the module, "Powered by [small-cut symbol] tenonfi" in the partner's muted text colour at 11 px. In monochrome, never in wood colours.
- **Not carried:** photography, the serif, patterns (except the hatch), the two-species logo.

## 4. API docs (`/docs`, light default, dark available)

- **Layout (swiss-minimalist structure):** two columns. Prose in Plex Sans on the left, request and response examples in Plex Mono on the right on `paper-sunk`. A 12-column lattice with P1-coarse hairlines in the page margins only. P2 stepped dividers at the end of each section.
- **Provenance in the schema:** every numeric example shows its `source`, `fetched_at` and `method` fields, and the pin glyph annotates them in rendered examples.
- **Mock examples:** hatched code-block gutter + `"provenance": "mock"` highlighted + the word MOCK.
- **Disclaimer:** the same constant, in a hairline box at the top of the reference section.
- **Header:** symbol + `tenonfi` + "API" in Plex Sans. No photography, no serif in the body.

## 5. Tenonfi Bearing dashboard (dark default)

- **Register:** an instrument drawn as a drawing, not hardware. `black` ground with `char` and `char-2` layers and hairline rows. No bevels, no nameplates, no LED colours (anti-Teiten).
- **Type:** Plex Sans Condensed 12–13 px for tables, Plex Mono for values. No serif and no photography in the working view.
- **Hero visual:** the hour-of-week depth **heatmap as a P1 lattice** (24 × 7), lightness ramp only, no-sample cells hatched.
- **Every metric:** unit · n= · method version · as-of (UTC) · pin. Example: "xAAPL · $1.2M sellable at ≤ 2% impact · Sun 03:00 UTC · n=412 · method v1.3 ⊡".
- **Lockup:** symbol + `tenonfi Bearing`, top left, at a modest size.
- **Bearing landing page (not the dashboard):** may use H4b (bracket-set render) with P3 behind the title only.
