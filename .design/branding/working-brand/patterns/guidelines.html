03 Color

One material. Two species. Swapped by the ground.

Kumiko and lattice as structure, never ornament.

P1 coarse kumiko. The layout grid made visible: marketing section grounds, and docs page margins only. On a ::before layer, never on a content box. It stops one grid unit short of any figure.
