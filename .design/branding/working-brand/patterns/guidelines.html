05 Visual elements

Joints as drawings. Light as a gradient. The hatch as a word.

Five joints carry the metaphor, drawn as line work: lines in the text colour, the key member in honey, dimension lines in chalk. Patterns never sit behind numbers, text, form fields, charts, the provenance popover or the disclaimer. Only the hatch is diagonal, and the hatch means sample (or stale).

The logo, the plan, provenance. Post = the goal, tenon = the plan cut to fit, pin = the source.

Structure and measurement: the unit cell of the lattice, the layout grid, the heatmap.
J3 · Sliding dovetail, dropped in
The exit plan. The way out is the way in, reversed, designed first. The lift-out path is dashed.

Drawings are inline SVG with currentColor and CSS variables, 1.5 px strokes, square caps, so the theme swap is free. Figures in drawings are HTML overlays, never SVG text, so the pin and its popover work. Not in the set: furniture dovetails, puzzle boxes, screws, nails, glue.

P1 lattice. The layout grid made visible as a faint hairline (line / line-l at 60%), on marketing section grounds only. On a ::before layer, never on a content box, never in the app, Bearing or the embed. It stops one grid unit short of any figure.
